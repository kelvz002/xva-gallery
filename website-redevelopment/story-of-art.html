The Story of Art

A series of art history lectures headed by Dr. Michaela Watrelot exploring the highlights of different art movements, the most influential artists and their masterpieces. These lectures provide a foundation in art history from the 19th Century to the present day.

Lecture 01

The Age of Revolution - Classicism to Realism

The times leading to the 1789 French Revolution provided artists with enormous impulse. Follow Delacroix, Goya, Turner, Constable and Courbet through the movements that made reality an artistic subject.

Lecture 02

The power of Light - Impressionism

Explore the new treatment of colour and movement developed by Manet, Monet, Pissarro, Renoir and Degas as the Impressionists abandoned the studio and followed the light.

Lecture 03

On the path to Modern Art - Post Impressionism

Meet Seurat, Cézanne, Gauguin and Van Gogh, whose work marked a turning point in the relationship between colour, form, perspective and emotion.

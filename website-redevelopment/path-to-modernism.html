Path to Modernism

Join us at XVA Gallery for an intensive art day with the Impressionists. In line with the ongoing exhibition at Louvre Abu Dhabi, explore the movements and works by Manet, Monet, Pissarro, Renoir, Sisley, Degas, Morisot and Gustave Caillebotte.

Saturday 17th December

A day with the Impressionists.

10:00 - 11:15 / Manet and the forming of the movement
11:15 - 11:30 / Coffee break
11:30 - 13:00 / Impressionism, late works
13:00 - 14:00 / Lunch and art discussion

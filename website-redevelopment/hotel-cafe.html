XVA Hotel

A retreat in the maze-like sikkas of Al Fahidi.

The Al Fahidi Neighborhood, formerly known as Al Bastakiya, is Dubai's oldest area and a former prominent trader's settlement, near the Dubai Fort. Hidden in the heart of the historical Al Fahidi, XVA Art Hotel is the former home of the Seddiqi family, turned boutique hotel in the early 2000s by founder Mona Hauser.

XVA Hotel

Art, culture, heritage and creativity.

Offering 15 individually designed suites and rooms, an award-winning restaurant, wind towers and three courtyards, this centrally located hotspot is the city's unique retreat.

XVA Café

Dubai's best kept secret.

The café serves freshly prepared vegetarian and vegan food including main courses, salads, soups and desserts. Enjoy a refreshing mint lemonade in the green and cozy courtyard, away from the bustle of the city.

Open daily from 7 am to 10 pm.
